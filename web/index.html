NOTIFICATIONS

Phone alerts

Receive new stove faults and warnings even when the app is closed. Enable notifications separately on each device.

Estimated pellet stock triggers alerts below 50%, 20% and 10%. No alert is sent at exactly 50%. Consumption is counted in kilograms, so the estimate can jump from 50% to about 47%. Confirming a refill rearms the relevant alerts.

Ash drawer reminders begin after you log two emptyings. Log an emptying in the Status tab.
