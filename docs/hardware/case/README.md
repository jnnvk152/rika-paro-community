# Example enclosure

Author: **broodforce**. Source: [Thingiverse 6640089](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6640089). Licence: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

`TOP.stl` and `BOTTOM.stl` are unchanged originals. Print at 100% scale in millimetres. The original profile suggests 0.16 mm layers, 15% infill, no supports or raft; orient the flat outer face on the bed.

The dimensional check compares a nominal 62.74 × 25.40 × 1.60 mm board against a 62.94 × 25.60 mm PCB recess (0.10 mm per side). USB shells and cut-outs are included in the accompanying measurements. **A printed physical fit has not been verified.** Check your exact board revision, connectors and tolerances before printing. The fit claim is not universal across clones.
